Privee is an anonymous, end-to-end encrypted chat.

<ul>
<li>No phone number, email or contact list: pick a session name (or let the app generate one) and share it.</li>
<li>Messages are encrypted on your device with the Signal protocol (PQXDH key agreement and the Double Ratchet), so the server only relays ciphertext.</li>
<li>Safety numbers let you verify who you are talking to, and you are warned when a contact's identity changes.</li>
<li>Chats with people using the Privee website work seamlessly.</li>
<li>Push notifications through UnifiedPush, with no Google services required.</li>
<li>No central server: connect to any Privee server, including one you host yourself.</li>
</ul>
